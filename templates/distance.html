Distance Sales Agreement

Issued under Turkish Law No. 6502 on Consumer Protection and the Distance Contracts Regulation.

Article 1 — Parties

Seller details are listed above. The buyer is the natural or legal person who places an order on this website.

Article 2 — Subject

This agreement sets out the rights and obligations for the sale and delivery of products ordered electronically on marvispace.com.

Article 3 — Product and price

  • Product features and images are shown on the product page
  • Prices include VAT unless stated otherwise
  • Payment is taken by credit or debit card with 3D Secure
  • Any cargo fee is shown in the order summary

Article 4 — Delivery

Goods are delivered to the address you provide by a contracted cargo company. Estimated delivery is 3–7 business days after order confirmation; stock or force majeure may extend this.

Article 5 — Right of withdrawal

You may withdraw within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. See Returns.

Withdrawal may not apply to made-to-order, hygiene-sensitive, or customized leather goods (Regulation Art. 15).

Article 6 — Disputes

Consumers may apply to Consumer Arbitration Committees and Consumer Courts. Monetary thresholds are updated yearly by the Ministry of Trade.
