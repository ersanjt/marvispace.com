KVKK Privacy Notice

Official privacy notice under Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK). MARVISPACE is the data controller.

1. Personal data we process

  • Identity and contact: name, email, phone, address
  • Order and payment: order details, invoice data, payment verification records (card details are not stored on our servers)
  • Security: IP, browser, session, and 3D Secure logs
  • Marketing (with consent): newsletter signups

2. Purpose and legal basis

  • Forming and performing the contract (distance sales, delivery, invoicing)
  • Payment provider and bank integration, fraud prevention
  • Legal obligations (consumer law, e-commerce, tax)
  • Marketing communications with your explicit consent

3. Transfers

Your data may be shared with payment providers (Ziraat Bankası Virtual POS, iyzico/Paynet), cargo companies, e-invoice providers, and public authorities when required by law. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Retention

Order and accounting records are kept for the periods required by law; marketing records until you withdraw consent.

5. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

  • Learn whether your data is processed
  • Request correction, deletion, or anonymization
  • Object to processing and claim compensation for damage

Send requests to support@marvispace.com. We reply within 30 days. support@marvispace.com
