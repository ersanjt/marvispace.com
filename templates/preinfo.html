Pre-contract Information

Required information before a distance sale, under the Distance Contracts Regulation Art. 5.

1. Product / service

Orders cover leather apparel and accessories shown on this site with images and descriptions. Core features are listed on each product page.

2. Total price

  • Product price: unit price in the cart × quantity
  • Cargo fee: shown in the order summary when it applies
  • VAT: included in prices unless stated otherwise
  • Amount payable: shown on the checkout screen

3. Payment and delivery

  • Payment: credit or debit card with 3D Secure
  • Payment providers: Ziraat Bankası Virtual POS and/or iyzico (Paynet)
  • Delivery: Turkey cargo to the buyer’s address
  • Estimated time: 3–7 business days from Istanbul

4. Right of withdrawal

You may withdraw within 14 days of delivery. Return terms: Returns

5. Complaints

Send complaints to support@marvispace.com or our phone number. You may also apply to Consumer Arbitration Committees and Consumer Courts. support@marvispace.com
