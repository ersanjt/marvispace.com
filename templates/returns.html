Returns & Withdrawal

Consumer rights and the return process under Turkish Law No. 6502.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

You may withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receiving the product, without giving a reason.

  • Send your withdrawal notice to support@marvispace.com
  • The product must be unused, with tags attached, in its original packaging
  • Return shipping costs are applied in line with applicable law when you use the right of withdrawal

Products excluded from withdrawal

  • Products customized to the consumer’s specifications
  • Goods that cannot be returned for hygiene reasons once the packaging is opened (where applicable)
  • Discounted / outlet items sold with a notice that withdrawal does not apply

Return process

  1. Email your withdrawal request (order number + full name)
  2. We send the return address and shipping instructions
  3. After the product arrives at our warehouse, we refund within 14 days
  4. The refund goes to the card or account you paid with (bank times may vary)

Damaged or defective goods

If you find damage on delivery, have the courier record a report and notify us with photos within 48 hours.
